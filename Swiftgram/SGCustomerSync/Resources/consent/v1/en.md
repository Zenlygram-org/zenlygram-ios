## 2. What we collect and why

**A. Only with this consent:**

- **Phone number** of this Telegram account, as the app knows it from your Telegram session: to find your record when you contact our support, and to count users by country (only the country code is used for statistics).
- **Username or usernames** of this account: to find your record when you contact our support.
- **First and last name** of this account: to address you and to find your record when you contact our support.
- **Language:** your Telegram language and the language of the app, so that our messages reach you in your language, and for statistics.
- **Telegram Premium:** only whether you have it (yes or no), for statistics.
- **Device and app details:** device type (phone or tablet), app build, where the app was installed from, region format and time zone: for support and for statistics.
- **Usage statistics:** when you open and close the app and for how long; which Zenlygram screens you open (screen names only; of Telegram's own screens only the main tabs, never a chat); which Zenlygram features you use (for example tasks, the secret vault, chat lock and edit history) and which of its settings you switch; the steps of buying a subscription (opening the Pro screen, choosing a plan, starting a purchase, success, failure, restoring); whether linking the account worked; the number of errors (error type and code only). We use it to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
- **Your to-do lists (cloud tasks):** list and task titles, descriptions, due dates, priority, order and completion; for shared lists, the Telegram IDs of the people you invite; for assignees you add, the Telegram ID and name you enter. We use it to keep your tasks in sync on your devices and to share the lists you choose. After you agree, the tasks you already have in Zenlygram are copied to your cloud tasks once, automatically. On iPhone and iPad, the tasks saved on this device go to the first account on the device that agrees. While the account is linked, new tasks are kept in the cloud.

**B. For the account link and the subscription.** The app sends these when it links this account by itself after you agree. Without this consent it sends them only when you link this account yourself or buy or restore a subscription, because the link and the subscription cannot work without them:

- **Telegram ID** (the number of your Telegram account): to link this account to your Zenlygram account, so that your subscription, your cloud tasks and the messages of our bot belong to you on all your devices.
- **Device link data:** a random device identifier, the app's public signing key, platform (iOS or Android), device model, operating system version, app version and, if notifications are on, a notification token: to keep the account link secure, to show you your devices and to deliver notifications; with this consent also for support and statistics.
- **Purchases:** if you buy or restore a subscription, the App Store or Google Play purchase data: to verify the purchase.

With every request, including the app's anonymous check whether Zenlygram Pro is free or paid, our server also sees the IP address and the app's technical identification string (user agent). We use them only to protect the service from abuse; section 4 says how long security records and server logs keep them.

We never collect: the text or any other content of your messages, chats and channels; your contacts and address book; the IDs or names of the chats, people, groups and channels you talk to (except what you write into a cloud task yourself); your location (we do not use GPS or your IP address to find where you are); advertising identifiers; what you type or search for; your photos, videos, voice messages, files and calls. We do not use data from Telegram to train or run artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

## 3. Who receives your data and where it is kept

- We keep your data on our own server in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. Your phone number, usernames and names are stored encrypted, with keys that are not kept in the database.
- **Our hosting provider** (a data centre in Tashkent): runs our server for us and does not use the data for its own purposes.
- **Telegram** (Bot API): when our bot sends you a message (for example a receipt or a reminder), Telegram receives your Telegram ID and the message.
- **Apple** (App Store and Apple Push Notification service): purchase identifiers, to verify App Store purchases, and the notification token, to deliver notifications to iPhone and iPad.
- **Google** (Google Play and Firebase Cloud Messaging): purchase data, to verify Google Play purchases, and the notification token, to deliver notifications to Android devices; a notification carries no message content, only a random identifier.
- **Our partner's subscription bot @ZenlyPayBot**, if this check is turned on: your Telegram ID, only to find out whether you already pay for a subscription there.

Telegram, Apple and Google are located outside Uzbekistan. By agreeing, you also agree to this cross-border transfer, for these purposes only. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not publish it.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Profile (phone number, usernames, first and last name, language, Telegram Premium, device details): until you withdraw this consent or delete your Zenlygram account.
- Account link (Telegram ID and linked devices): until you delete your Zenlygram account; a device that has not been used for 180 days is unlinked.
- Detailed usage statistics: 180 days.
- Days of activity and daily totals: 25 months; the daily totals do not name anyone.
- Cloud tasks: until you delete them, withdraw this consent or delete your Zenlygram account. The content of a deleted task or list is erased within 3 days; invitations of a deleted task, with the invited person's Telegram ID, are deleted within 30 days.
- Security records with IP addresses: 180 days; account-link requests: 30 days.
- Encrypted backups and server logs: 14 days; data deleted from the server can stay in them until then.
- The record of this consent: while your Zenlygram account exists and for 3 years after it is deleted; after deletion it keeps only the account number, the version of this text and the dates.
- Payment records: as long as tax and accounting rules require; when you delete your account, the data in them that identifies you is removed.

This consent is valid until you withdraw it or delete your Zenlygram account.

## 5. Your rights and how to use them

- **To know:** see your profile, devices, consents, activity, statistics and subscription in Settings → Zenlygram → Data & consent → My data; for anything else we hold about you, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.
- **To withdraw consent:** at any time in Settings → Zenlygram → Data & consent, or by writing to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Once your withdrawal reaches our server, we delete your profile, statistics and cloud tasks no later than the next working day. The link between this Telegram account and your Zenlygram account stays until you delete the account, because your subscription depends on it.
- **To delete:** delete your Zenlygram account and its data in Settings → Zenlygram → Data & consent → Delete my Zenlygram account and data, or on the page [zenlygram.org/account/delete](https://zenlygram.org/account/delete). Deleting does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel it there.
- **To correct or stop:** ask us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} to correct your data, or to stop processing data that is inaccurate, outdated or no longer needed.
- **To complain:** to the authorised state body for personal data (the State Personalization Centre under the Cabinet of Ministers of the Republic of Uzbekistan) or to a court.

What happens in the app if you do not agree or withdraw this consent is shown on the screen before you confirm.

## 6. Confirming your account in the background

To link this Telegram account to Zenlygram without a code, the app asks Telegram, in the background and as this account, for a confirmation from our bot's Mini App. Telegram then gives our bot your public profile (Telegram ID, first and last name, username, language and whether you have Telegram Premium) and whether you allow our bot to message you. With this consent we keep it as described above; without it we keep only your Telegram ID. We never keep your profile photo. The app does this by itself only after you agree, and only for the account you agreed for; it may also do this when you link this account yourself. It never sends /start or any other message to the bot, never opens the Mini App on your screen, never shares your contact card and never accepts a login request on your behalf. If you link with a code instead, our bot receives the same public profile when you confirm in the bot.
